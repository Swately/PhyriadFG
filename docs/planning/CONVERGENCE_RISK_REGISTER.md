2. **The M4 veto is a gate, not a risk row:** C3a / C3b do not close without the T6 byte-diff record
   (strategies X9) — a stage that "looks identical" is not closed.
3. **The M1 gate is blocked, never proxied (XR9):** C4 may be built; it is not `done` until MOTION_TRUTH
   T4–T5 are `measured`.

   MINIMAL_FG); the session prepares evidence, never flips.
5. **Adoption is now the seam header + its test only** (S4.0, operator decision 2026-09-03); the app
   `apps/minimal_fg` is not adopted and no stage is blocked on a relocation (XR10 re-scoped).
